## 📌 My Approach

I believe that learning IT is not just about watching tutorials or memorizing concepts. It's also about applying what I learn, testing different configurations, solving problems, and understanding why things work.

For every meaningful lab or project, I aim to document:

- **Objective** — What I want to accomplish
- **Setup** — Tools, topology, or environment used
- **Implementation** — Steps and configurations
- **Testing** — How I verify that everything works
- **Troubleshooting** — Problems encountered and how I solved them
- **Key Takeaways** — What I learned from the experience
